Welcome to God's Care

Welcome to God's Care Community School Banda, a trusted community school founded on Christian values and a passion for excellence. Located in the heart of Banda Zone B1, Acholi Quarters, we provide a warm, safe, and stimulating environment where every child is valued, nurtured, and inspired to excel.


We believe education is more than books — it is about building character, instilling Godly morals, developing talents, and preparing children for a bright future. From Baby Class to Primary Seven, our pupils receive holistic training that balances academics, discipline, spiritual growth, and co-curricular activities.


At God's Care, With God We Excel is not just our motto, it is our daily reality.

📚 Academic Excellence

Well-structured curriculum, regular assessments, remedial support, and focused PLE preparation to ensure every learner performs to their best ability.

👩‍🏫 Dedicated Teachers

Qualified, experienced, and compassionate teachers who understand children and give individual attention to help each child grow at their own pace.

🏫 Day & Boarding Facility

Clean, secure, and well-supervised boarding section with caring matrons, balanced meals, and a homely environment for our boarders.

🎭 Talents & Skills

We nurture talents through Music Dance & Drama, football, netball, debate, Scripture Union, and clubs to build confidence and leadership.

🛡️ Safe & Caring Environment

Fenced and secure compound, disciplined yet loving atmosphere, clean water, good sanitation, and strict child safety policies.

🙏 Christian Foundation

Morning devotions, Christian values, respect, honesty, and hard work are taught daily to shape children into responsible citizens.

Why Parents Trust Us

Parents across Banda, Kireka, Kyambogo, and Acholi Quarters trust us because we listen, we care, and we deliver results. We maintain close communication with parents, we have small class sizes for better learning, and our compound is always clean and orderly. Your child’s future is safe with us.
