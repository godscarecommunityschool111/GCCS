Welcome to God's Care

God's Care Community School is a mixed day and boarding nursery and primary school located in Banda Zone B1, Acholi Quarters, off Kinawataka Road, Kampala, Uganda.

We aim to provide a supportive environment where every child can develop academically, socially, and morally.
