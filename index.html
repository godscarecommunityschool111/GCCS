Welcome to God's Care

God's Care Community School is a mixed day and boarding nursery and primary school founded by Rose Lwazi Batenga, the Director. Located in Banda Zone B1, Acholi Quarters, off Kinawataka Road, Kampala, Uganda.

We aim to provide a supportive environment where every child can develop academically, socially, and morally.

📚

Quality Learning

Encouraging curiosity, knowledge, creativity, and a love of learning.

🤝
